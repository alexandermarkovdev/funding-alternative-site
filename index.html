Your questions, answered

A few things to know before you apply.

How much can I borrow?

Between £15,000 and £250,000.

How quickly can you lend?

We give an indicative offer within 4 hours. Once you accept, we aim to fund within 24 hours of receiving all the information we need.

Do I need to be a homeowner?

Yes. At least one director or shareholder needs to be a UK homeowner.

What information do I need to get an offer?

From the start, we'll ask for:

  • 6 months of company bank statements, or Open Banking for a quicker answer (we need data for all related company accounts)
  • The amount you need, and what it's for
  • The address of a UK homeowner director or shareholder
  • A brief description of how your business makes money
What do repayments look like?

You can choose fixed repayments, or flexible repayments where we take a percentage of your receipts plus a minimum weekly amount.

Repayment terms are typically between 3 and 9 months.

What is an early repayment discount?

If you repay before the end of the term, you can receive a discount on the amount you owe.
