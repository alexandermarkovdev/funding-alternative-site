What is a Merchant Cash Advance?

Explained

A Merchant Cash Advance is a flexible financing solution designed to support a wide range of businesses. It's easier to qualify for than a traditional loan, making it ideal for companies that need extra capital to grow, even if they have poor credit, limited assets, or operate in industries that often struggle to access funding.

With flexible options and quick access to funds, a Merchant Cash Advance is a convenient choice for small and medium-sized businesses.

In short: an amount paid to your business, repaid through your daily card sales over a variable time frame (typically 9 months, depending on your card sales) with a fixed fee. Advances range from £15,000 up to £250,000.

Can my business apply?

  • You're a limited company, sole trader or partnership
  • You're registered in England, Wales or Scotland
  • You have over £15,000 average monthly revenue
  • There are no bankruptcies or disqualifications
  • Card terminal revenue is evident in your cash flow

What you'll need

  • Company name and registration number
  • 6 months of company bank statements, or Open Banking
  • The amount you need and the reason for the funds
  • Homeownership details (address and mortgage value)
  • Your card terminal supplier and any outstanding balances with existing MCAs

How do my repayments work?

Repayments

With a Merchant Cash Advance, repayments are designed to match your cash flow.

Revenue-based repayments

A small, agreed percentage is automatically taken from your daily card sales. On busy days you pay a little more, and on slower days you pay less.
