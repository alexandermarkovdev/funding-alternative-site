About our funding FAQs

Our FAQ page is designed to give British business owners clear, straightforward answers on everything. From how much you can borrow and how quickly you can get it, to what we look at when assessing your cash flow and how repayments work.

We keep things simple and transparent, so you can understand your options in minutes. You decide whether a short‑term, unsecured cash flow advance from Funding Alternative is right for your business.

Borrowing

How much can I borrow?

Between £15,000 and £250,000.

How quickly can you lend?

We give an indicative offer within 4 hours. Once you accept, we aim to fund within 24 hours of receiving all the information we need.

Can you top up my funding?

Yes. Our analysts can reassess your business and extend additional funds.

What is an early repayment discount?

If you repay before the end of the term, you can receive a discount on the amount you owe.

What products do you offer?

We offer three products to British businesses:

  • Business Cash Advance
  • Selective Invoice Finance
  • Merchant Cash Advance

Who can apply

Do I need to be a homeowner?

Yes. At least one director or shareholder needs to be a UK homeowner.

Can you lend to sole traders and LLPs?

Yes, we can. Our minimum advance to sole traders is £25,000.

Do you lend to my sector?

We're sector agnostic, so we'll look at any business.

Do you lend to businesses outside the UK?

Not at the moment. We currently lend to businesses in England, Scotland and Wales.

Applying

What information do I need to get an offer?

From the start, we'll ask for:

  • 6 months of company bank statements, or Open Banking for a quicker answer (we need data for all related company accounts)
  • The amount you need, and what it's for
  • The address of a UK homeowner director or shareholder
  • A brief description of how your business makes money
Which banks work with Open Banking?

Most UK banks do. See the full list of supported institutions.

Repayments and security

What do repayments look like?

You can choose fixed repayments, or flexible repayments where we take a percentage of your receipts plus a minimum weekly amount.

How long do I have to repay?

Typically between 3 and 9 months.

What security do I need to provide?

All directors provide a personal guarantee. In some circumstances we also take a debenture.
